Discord Conduct
Behaviour expectations across all channels
⌄
1.1
Respect all membersWarning
Treat every member with respect regardless of rank, experience, or background. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind are strictly prohibited. This aligns with both Discord and Roblox TOS.
1.2
No inappropriate contentBan
Do not post NSFW content, graphic imagery, hate speech, or anything that violates Discord's Terms of Service or Community Guidelines. This is a Roblox-affiliated server — keep all content appropriate for all ages.
1.3
Use correct channels
Post content only in the relevant channel. For example, flight logs belong in #flight-logs, not in #general. Repeated off-topic posting may result in a warning.
1.4
No spam or excessive pingingWarning
Do not spam messages, use excessive caps lock, or repeatedly ping members or staff without reason. Pinging @everyone or @here is reserved for staff only.
1.5
No advertisingKick
Advertising other Discord servers, Roblox groups, or any external services without explicit staff permission is prohibited. This includes DM advertising to members.
1.6
English only in main channels
To ensure staff can properly moderate, please use English in all main channels. If there is sufficient demand, language-specific channels may be created.

In-Game Flying & Operations
Standards for all pilots and crew in PTFS
⌄
2.1
Realistic flying standards
All pilots are expected to fly in a realistic and professional manner. This includes correct taxiing, takeoff, cruise, and landing procedures. Reckless flying such as aerobatics during operations or intentional crashes is not permitted.
2.2
Follow ATC instructionsWarning
When ATC is active, all pilots must follow their instructions. Ignoring ATC clearances, taxiing onto active runways without clearance, or departing without permission is a violation of standard operations.
2.3
Use assigned aircraft only
Only fly aircraft you are rated or cleared to operate within the airline. Using aircraft outside your rank or clearance — particularly during official flights — is not permitted.
2.4
No trolling or griefingBan
Deliberately disrupting flights, ramming other aircraft, blocking runways, or interfering with other players' roleplay is strictly prohibited and may result in a permanent ban.
2.5
File a flight plan
Before conducting an official TUI flight, a flight plan should be submitted in the appropriate Discord channel. This includes your route, aircraft type, and estimated departure time.
2.6
Maintain radio communication
Stay active on the designated voice or text comms channel during flights. Going silent mid-flight without notice can disrupt operations for other members.
2.7
Log your flights
All completed official flights must be logged in the flight log channel with the required information (route, aircraft, duration). Unlogged flights will not count toward rank progression.

Staff & Roles
Rank conduct, promotions and expectations
⌄
3.1
Respect staff decisions
Staff decisions are to be respected. If you disagree with a moderation action, you may appeal it privately through the proper channels — do not argue publicly in channels or stir up drama.
3.2
No role impersonationBan
Claiming to be a staff member, moderator, or any role you do not hold — whether in-game or on Discord — is strictly prohibited. This includes mimicking staff names or nicknames.
3.3
Roles must be earned
Rank promotions are based on activity, flight logs, conduct, and staff review. Asking staff for a promotion repeatedly is discouraged — show your dedication through your work.
3.4
Staff conduct expectations
Staff members are held to a higher standard. Any abuse of moderation powers, favouritism, or misconduct by staff should be reported to senior staff or management via a direct message.
3.5
Inactivity policy
Members who are inactive for an extended period without notice may have their roles reviewed or reset. If you need a break, simply let staff know in the appropriate channel.
📅
Events & Flights
Participation rules for hosted events
⌄
4.1
RSVP and show up
If you RSVP to an event, please attend. Repeated no-shows without notifying staff in advance may result in losing priority access to future events or a role review.
4.2
Follow event host instructions
During an event, the designated event host is in charge. Their instructions take priority. If you have concerns, raise them after the event — not during.
4.3
No event disruptionKick
Deliberately ruining an event — through trolling, ignoring instructions, or griefing other participants — will result in immediate removal from the event and a potential server kick or ban.
4.4
Be ready before the event
Join the game server and have your uniform, aircraft, and livery set before the event begins. Causing delays because you're not prepared is inconsiderate to other members.
4.5
Event slots are first-come, first-served
When event capacity is limited, slots are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis unless a role entitles priority boarding. Do not pressure staff to reserve slots for you.
